On-farm demonstration – Materiel Agricole Gauthier (summer 2026)

From July to August 2026, I demonstrated the new New Holland T7.300 tractor and the BigBaler 1290 Plus directly on the customers' farms. This video was taken during one of these demonstrations, where I drive the tractor and the customer also tries it: the baler picks up the windrows and leaves the square bales behind it in the field.

A demonstration is the moment when the customer sees the machine working in their own field, with their own crop. I ran these demos for 20 customers, which led to 4 contracts.
