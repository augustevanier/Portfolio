Biography

I am Auguste Vanier, currently a Master of Science student in Mechanical Engineering at Stanford University (2026-2028). It is a double degree with the Arts et Métiers Institute of Technology (ENSAM): instead of my third and last year of the combined BSc-MSc in Mechanical and Industrial Engineering at Arts et Métiers, I am following the master program of Stanford. Through my schooling I had the opportunity to take part in various programs, projects and activities that forged my willingness to take the opportunity to become an engineer

I always wanted to work in the agricultural world, that, it was unavoidable. I grew up in fields, visiting farms next to my house, taking ears of wheat to smell the Orris butter scent and then I worked with all the agricultural machinery every summer since I was 16. My various sports experiences were as much experiences that crafted me and helped me to develop useful skills such as perseverance, analytic, synthetic and managing skills.

However, for years now, agricultural concerns about the rising of the needs and the difficulty to respond to it led me to want to push the limits of agricultural machinery to avoid a collapse of a world already on the edge of starvation.
