"SnowBoost" Project (60 hrs)

This was my first academic project, carried out in high school. It had to answer a general theme set by the Engineering Science Olympiad committee: Transport. With my team of three, we quickly decided to design a device able to help snowboarders move when they are stuck on a flat surface, because we had all been confronted with this problem.

During this project, we designed a 2-part track in 3D, in which we inserted metal plates to ensure the traction of the device. In a previous study, we tested the friction coefficient of various polymer materials and concluded that the most important criterion was the geometry. Then we sized the DC motor to provide the torque and speed required by our technical specifications. Once the motor was sized, we calculated the battery capacity needed for the 4-hour autonomy specification, and insulated the battery from the cold to keep its performance at its best. Finally, we built a remote control to set the speed of the motor, to which we added an LCD screen showing the slope and the battery level.

Thanks to our hard work, we reached the final of the regional Olympiad.
